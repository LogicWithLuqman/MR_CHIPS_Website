Our Story

The Mr Chips Story

Since 2010, Mr Chips has been serving Potchefstroom's Mohadin community with the freshest, most flavourful fast food in town. What started as a small chip shop has grown into a local favourite — known for our signature chicken tikka, perfectly grilled wors rolls, and chips that keep our customers coming back.

Every meal is prepared fresh, seasoned to perfection, and served with a smile. We believe great food doesn't have to be complicated — just quality ingredients, the right spices, and a whole lot of heart.
