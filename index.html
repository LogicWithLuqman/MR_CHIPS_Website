Our story

The Mr Chips Story

Since 2010, Mr Chips has been serving Potchefstroom's Mohadin community fresh chips, combos, chicken and cold drinks. What started as a small chip shop has grown into a local favourite built on quick service and honest prices.

Pickup is simple: choose your items, add them to your order, and send the order through WhatsApp. We keep the menu focused on the food our customers come back for.
